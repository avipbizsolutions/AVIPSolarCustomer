AVIP Solar app – Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

This policy explains what the AVIP Solar Android app (package com.avip.solarcustomer) collects and how it is used. The app is published by AVIP Business Solutions LLP (“AVIP Solar”, “we”), AIIMS–Naubatpur New Nahar Road, Patna – 801109, Bihar, India. It is for customers who have bought a rooftop solar system from AVIP Solar, to follow their installation, see their documents, refer friends and raise service requests.

The app does not read your contacts, location, SMS, call log or other apps, and has no advertising or analytics trackers. The camera is opened only when you tap to take a photo.

2. How the information is used and shared

  • Only AVIP Solar staff (office, sales, installation and service teams) use it, to deliver and service your solar system and pay referral rewards.
  • Information about your referred friend is used only to contact them about AVIP Solar.
  • We do not sell your information and do not share it for advertising.
  • It is stored in Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive) used by AVIP Solar; Google acts as our service provider.
  • We may share information with DISCOM / PM Surya Ghar portal authorities, banks or the equipment maker only when needed for your subsidy, loan, net meter or warranty, or when the law requires it.

3. Security

  • All data between the app and our server travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access is protected by your mobile number and a 4-digit PIN; 5 wrong PINs lock login for 10 minutes.
  • Only authorised AVIP Solar staff can open the office records.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while you are our customer and use the app. Records needed for warranty, subsidy, net metering, taxes and billing are kept as long as the law requires. Uploaded photos and service requests are deleted when you delete your account (see below), unless they are part of such records.

5. Delete your account and data

In the app: tap the person (profile) icon at the top → Privacy → मेरा account delete करें (Delete my account). Your app account is switched off at once.

Without the app: see Delete your AVIP Solar account.

Within 30 days we delete your app data: uploaded photos and documents, service requests, referral details and UPI ID. Records that the law requires us to keep (warranty, subsidy, net meter, invoices and taxes) are kept for the required period.

6. Your choices

  • You can turn off app notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • You can ask us to correct your details or for a copy of them by contacting us.
  • The app is meant for adults (18+) who are AVIP Solar customers. It is not directed at children.
